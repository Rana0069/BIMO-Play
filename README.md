# BIMO

**Modern Android music player created and maintained by Rana.**

BIMO is an Android music player focused on a clean modern interface, music playback, background playback, playlists, local music, queue management, and supported online music providers.

## Features

- Music playback
- Music search
- Background playback
- Lock-screen controls
- Android MediaSession controls
- Queue management
- Favorites
- Playlists
- Lyrics where supported
- Local music playback
- Material 3 interface
- AMOLED-friendly dark interface
- Android Auto where supported
- Online music providers where supported

## Music Providers

### YouTube / YouTube Music

BIMO supports YouTube/YouTube Music functionality where technically available.

Third-party services may change their APIs, access requirements, or availability.

### Spotify

BIMO may support Spotify through official Spotify mechanisms where supported.

### Local Music

BIMO supports compatible music files stored on the user's device.
